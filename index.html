350DIAS The Project

A new chapter of Bonfim

350 DIAS is a boutique residential development of six unique apartments created for contemporary urban living, just minutes from Porto's city center. Set within a traditional Porto building, the project combines the original architecture's character with a complete transformation of its interiors. Each apartment has been individually designed, featuring its own layout, proportions, natural light, exterior areas and architectural character, offering a sense of individuality rarely found in new developments.

Materials & Details

Contemporary living

Every home is designed for comfortable, everyday living, with carefully selected materials and finishes throughout. Custom-designed kitchens, wood flooring and marble countertops are complemented by quality fixtures, double-glazed thermal windows and high-performance insulation.

The Developers

ALBA URBANA is a real estate development company founded by Ido Ben Ari and Gil Aldor, bringing together complementary experience in investment, development, planning, and construction.

Ido has been active in real estate for over a decade in Israel and France, and manages a family-owned company that owns, develops, and manages properties across Tel Aviv, Paris, and Toulouse. Gil brings a background in design and construction, having previously owned a finishing and contracting business in Israel. Based in Portugal for the past four years, he has led residential and hospitality projects in central Porto.

Together, they focus on creating thoughtful urban projects that respect their surroundings and are designed for the way people live today.

ALBA URBANA is the trading name of ALBA TOP BONFIM, LDA.
