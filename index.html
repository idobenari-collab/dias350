350DIAS A project by ALBA URBANA

350DIAS brings new life to a traditional Porto building

Located in Bonfim, a neighborhood known for its local character and growing energy, the project creates six new apartments just minutes from the city centre.

The building is being completely redesigned from the inside, creating homes that make better use of space, light, and everyday functionality. No two apartments are the same — each one has its own layout, proportions, and character.

The Developers

A refined approach to urban living

ALBA URBANA is a real estate development company founded by Ido Ben Ari and Gil Aldor, bringing together complementary experience in investment, development, planning, and construction.

Ido has been active in real estate for over a decade in Israel and France, and manages a family-owned company that owns, develops, and manages properties across Tel Aviv, Paris, and Toulouse. Gil brings a background in design and construction, having previously owned a finishing and contracting business in Israel. Based in Portugal for the past four years, he has led residential and hospitality projects in central Porto.

Together, they focus on creating thoughtful urban projects that respect their surroundings and are designed for the way people live today.
